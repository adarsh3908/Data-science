# Stock Market Crash Analysis

## 📈 Project Overview
This project analyzes historical stock market data to identify patterns, triggers, and characteristics of market crashes. The analysis includes comprehensive statistical examination and visualization of market volatility during crisis periods.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze historical stock market crash patterns
- Identify early warning indicators of market instability
- Study volatility patterns during crisis periods
- Examine recovery patterns post-crash
- Develop insights for risk management

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source**: Historical stock market data and indices
- **Format**: Time-series financial data
- **Features**: Stock prices, volumes, market indices, volatility measures
- **Period**: Multiple market cycles including major crashes

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **Python**: Core programming language
- **Pandas**: Financial data manipulation
- **NumPy**: Statistical computations
- **Matplotlib**: Financial charting and visualization
- **Seaborn**: Advanced statistical plots
- **Financial Libraries**: Specialized financial analysis tools

## 📊 Analysis Framework
1. **Historical Analysis**: Major market crashes examination
2. **Volatility Analysis**: Risk and uncertainty measurements
3. **Technical Indicators**: Moving averages, RSI, Bollinger Bands
4. **Correlation Analysis**: Cross-market relationships
5. **Recovery Patterns**: Post-crash market behavior

## 📈 Key Components
- **Market Trend Analysis**: Long-term and short-term patterns
- **Crash Detection**: Identifying significant market drops
- **Risk Metrics**: VaR, volatility clustering, drawdowns
- **Comparative Analysis**: Different crashes and their characteristics
- **Predictive Indicators**: Early warning signals

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Open `Stock Market Crash Analysis.ipynb` in your notebook environment
2. Install required packages: `pip install pandas numpy matplotlib seaborn yfinance`
3. Load historical market data
4. Run analysis cells sequentially
5. Interpret visualizations and statistical results
